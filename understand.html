Understand Your Plant's Health

What do the results mean? Here's how to interpret them.

✅ Healthy

Your plant looks great! Its leaves are green and vibrant, indicating it's getting the right amount of sunlight, water, and nutrients.

Tip: Keep up the good care — regular watering, proper lighting, and occasional pruning will keep it thriving.

💧 Dry

Your plant might not be getting enough water. Dry or crispy leaves can be a sign of underwatering or very dry air.

What to do: Check the soil moisture. If it feels dry, water the plant thoroughly. Also consider misting or using a humidity tray.

🔥 Sunburned

Too much direct sunlight can cause brown or scorched patches on leaves. This means your plant might be getting sunburned.

What to do: Move your plant to a spot with indirect light or provide some shade. Trim affected leaves if needed.
